Only available in the Italian market.

BizVoIP is the free app for customers of Biztems' VoIP service: it brings your office extension to your smartphone.

• Answer your office calls wherever you are: they reach you even when the app is closed, just like a regular call.
• Call out with your company's number, just like from your desk phone.
• Protected calls: the connection to the PBX is encrypted (TLS), and so is your voice (SRTP).
• Your contacts and call history at hand.
• Set up in a moment: scan the QR code Biztems sent you.

BizVoIP works only with an account on Biztems' VoIP service: ask your company's administrator or Biztems for one.

Emergency calls: for 112 and other emergency numbers, use your phone's own Phone app.

BizVoIP is free software, based on Linphone by Belledonne Communications. Its source code is at github.com/biztems.
